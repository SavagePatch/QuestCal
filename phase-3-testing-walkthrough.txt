==============================================================
TEST 1: Player Availability Page
==============================================================

1. Sign in as a player (e.g. alice@questcal.dev / password123)
2. Click "My Availability" in the header nav
3. You should see a monthly calendar grid with:
   - 4 rows: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Late Night
   - Columns for each day of the current month
   - Some cells pre-filled from seed data (green = Available, amber = Maybe)
4. Click an empty cell -> it turns green (Available / "Y")
5. Click the green cell again -> it turns amber (Maybe / "?")
6. Click the amber cell again -> it clears back to empty
7. Use the left/right arrows to navigate months
   - Moving to a different month fetches that month's data
   - Returning to the current month should show your changes persisted
8. Verify the legend at the bottom shows all three states

WHAT TO CHECK:
- Cells update instantly (optimistic UI)
- Refreshing the page preserves your changes
- Different months load independently
- Weekend columns are slightly dimmed

==============================================================
TEST 2: GM Heatmap Page
==============================================================

1. Sign out, then sign in as GM (gm@questcal.dev / password123)
2. Click "Heatmap" in the header nav
3. You should see:
   - A campaign filter dropdown (defaults to "All Campaigns")
   - Month navigation arrows
   - A heat-colored grid showing aggregated availability
   - Cells display counts like "3/4" (3 of 4 players available)
4. Hover over a populated cell -> tooltip shows player names and statuses
5. Change the campaign filter dropdown:
   - "Curse of Strahd" -> shows data for 4 players (Alice, Bob, Charlie, Diana)
   - "Tomb of Annihilation" -> shows data for 3 players (Bob, Diana, Erik)
   - "All Campaigns" -> shows deduplicated data across all players
6. Navigate to different months
   - Months without seed data should show "No availability data"
   - Current month should have seed data

WHAT TO CHECK:
- Heat colors reflect coverage (green = all available, amber = some, gray = none)
- Tooltip correctly shows player names with yes/maybe distinction
- Campaign filter changes the grid immediately
- Player counts in cells match the campaign roster size

==============================================================
TEST 3: GM Players Page
==============================================================

1. While signed in as GM, click "Players" in the header nav
2. You should see a table with columns:
   - Name, Timezone, Campaigns, Slots (current month), Last Updated
3. Verify:
   - All 6 seed players appear (Alice, Bob, Charlie, Diana, Erik, Fumiko)
   - Timezones vary (New York, Chicago, Denver, Los Angeles, London, Tokyo)
   - Campaign column shows which campaigns each player belongs to
     (Bob and Diana should appear in both campaigns)
   - "Slots" column shows a green count (from seed data) for the current month
   - "Last Updated" column shows a date

WHAT TO CHECK:
- GM user does NOT appear in the player list
- Players are sorted alphabetically by name
- Slot counts match the seed data (randomized, so numbers will vary)

==============================================================
TEST 4: GM Campaigns Page
==============================================================

1. While signed in as GM, click "Campaigns" in the header nav
2. You should see two campaign cards:
   - "Curse of Strahd" with 4 player name chips
   - "Tomb of Annihilation" with 3 player name chips
3. Each card shows campaign description and player roster

SCHEDULE A SESSION:
4. On the "Curse of Strahd" card, click "Schedule Session"
5. An inline form appears with: Date, Time Block, Title, Notes
6. Fill in:
   - Date: pick any future date
   - Time Block: "Evening"
   - Title: "Session 1: Into Barovia"
   - Notes: (optional, leave blank or add text)
7. Click "Schedule"
8. The form collapses and the new session appears in the
   "Upcoming Sessions" section of the card
9. Click "Cancel" to dismiss the form without scheduling

WHAT TO CHECK:
- Session appears immediately after scheduling (no page refresh needed)
- Sessions are sorted by date
- The session shows: date, time block label, and title
- Scheduling another session on a different campaign works independently
- Refreshing the page shows the scheduled sessions persist
